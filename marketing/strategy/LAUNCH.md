# Launch Working Plan

**STATUS: RESEARCH REQUIRED**

## Gates

- Selected geography × category has auditable evidence.
- Required provider coverage, capacity, onboarding, and verification thresholds are defined and met.
- Analytics, support, dispute, pause, and rollback paths are ready.
- Demand activation fits verified capacity.
- Legal, Safety, Money/Action, and Red Team reviews are recorded.

Expansion is metric-gated, never scheduled solely by elapsed time.

No launch geography, category, date, or readiness threshold is approved. A launch brief must cover selected-market evidence, verified provider density, legitimate listings, instrumentation, demand plan, support capacity, trust/safety, contribution economics, stop/rollback conditions, owners, and human go/no-go approval. Calendar passage is not readiness.
