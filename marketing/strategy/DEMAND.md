# Demand Strategy

**STATUS: RESEARCH REQUIRED**

Activate demand only where verified supply can fulfill it. Prioritize local transactional intent, provider distribution, community relevance, customer proof, repeat use, and post-value referrals. Track search-to-booking, match/completion, time to match, cancellation/dispute, repeat, referral, and contribution margin.

Demand activation follows sufficient legitimate supply in the active geography × category. Prioritize qualified local intent and completed transactions over reach. Candidate channels, CAC, conversion, trust barriers, offers, and capacity constraints require evidence and bounded tests before scaling.
