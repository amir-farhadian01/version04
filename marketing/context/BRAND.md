# Brand

**STATUS: RESEARCH REQUIRED**

## Working principles

Be local, useful, credible, neighborly, inclusive, and clear. Avoid fabricated social proof, exaggerated availability, manipulative urgency, legal conclusions, or claims that outpace verified supply and product behavior.

## To establish

Voice attributes, terminology, accessibility/inclusion rules, visual references, claims policy, review/approval owners, and examples derived from founder-approved brand work.

## Interim guardrails

- Be specific, local, useful, truthful, and respectful.
- Do not claim safety, verification, savings, availability, market leadership, or guaranteed outcomes without current evidence and approval.
- Do not fabricate community/customer/provider stories or simulate grassroots support.
- Obtain permission for identifiable stories and assets.

Formal voice, visual, accessibility, claims, and crisis guidance require founder review.
