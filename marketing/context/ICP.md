# Ideal Customer and Provider Profiles

**STATUS: RESEARCH REQUIRED**

Do not assume a winning geography, category, customer segment, or provider type.

## Customer profile template

Job/need; geography; frequency/urgency; current alternative; trust burden; willingness to pay; acquisition channel; evidence/source/date.

## Provider profile template

Category; service radius; capacity; current channels; economics; operational maturity; verification readiness; acquisition friction; evidence/source/date.

No customer segment, provider segment, geography, or category is selected. Research must distinguish customer and provider jobs, eligibility, frequency, economics, trust burden, acquisition difficulty, and evidence limitations. Do not convert an interview hypothesis into an ICP conclusion.
